Programs

Structured pathways into research excellence.

Delivered through a deliberate hybrid model — digital platforms first for widest access, expanding to on-site programs as the Institute grows.

What We Offer

Six program pathways.

01

Research Mentorship & Training

Structured mentorship pairing students and early-career researchers with experienced scholars — covering research design, methodology, academic writing, and publication skills.
02

Fellowships & Internships

Research fellowships and internship opportunities that give students and early-career researchers hands-on experience in active research environments.
03

Capacity-Building Workshops & Bootcamps

Intensive workshops, seminars, and academic bootcamps that build practical research skills — from data analysis to grant writing and research ethics.
04

Conferences, Seminars & Symposiums

Scholarly forums for knowledge dissemination and research skill development — virtual first, expanding to on-site gatherings within Nigeria and beyond.
05

Collaborative Research & Innovation Projects

Multi-institution research projects and innovation initiatives addressing health, agricultural, technological, and societal challenges.
06

Knowledge Dissemination

Platforms for publishing and sharing research — journals, reports, policy briefs, and digital knowledge resources that make African research visible.

"Digital-first for reach. On-site for depth. A deliberate hybrid model — scalable from day one."
