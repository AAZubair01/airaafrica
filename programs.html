Programs

A plan, not a catalogue of empty offerings.

We list what we intend to build — and we mark clearly what is launching first versus what waits until we have people, process, and proof.

Programme Roadmap

Six pathways. One launch priority.

01 Launching first

Research Mentorship & Training

Structured pairing of students and early-career researchers with experienced scholars — research design, methodology, academic writing, and ethics. Remote delivery. Small first cohort.
02 After mentorship pilot

Capacity-Building Workshops & Bootcamps

Short, practical workshops on data analysis, grant writing, and research ethics — online first, then hybrid when we can host properly.
03 Later

Fellowships & Internships

Hands-on research placements require host labs, supervisors, and insurance of quality. We will not advertise these until partnerships exist.
04 Later

Conferences, Seminars & Symposiums

Scholarly forums for dissemination. Virtual seminars can start earlier; large on-site events wait for funding and organisational capacity.
05 Later

Collaborative Research & Innovation Projects

Multi-institution projects need MoUs, ethics clearance pathways, and shared budgets. Premature collaboration theatre helps no one.
06 Later

Knowledge Dissemination

Journals, reports, and policy briefs come after we have something real to publish — starting with mentorship outcomes and working papers.

"Digital-first for reach. On-site for depth. Proof before scale."

The AIRA Africa delivery model

Who It's For

Built for the next generation of African researchers.

  • Undergraduate and postgraduate students ready for structured guidance
  • Early-career researchers who need writing and methods support
  • Experienced scholars willing to mentor remotely
  • Universities and departments open to joint pilots with clear MoUs
