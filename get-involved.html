Get Involved

We need builders, not spectators.

AIRA Africa is in its founding phase. There is no paid staff, no equipment budget, and no office yet. If you join, you are helping create the Institute — not joining a finished organisation.

Four Pathways

Pick a lane. Be specific.

Vague offers of “help” are hard to use. Tell us what you can do, how many hours per week, and for how long.

Become a Member

Membership is by application and Board approval. Members form the community that will shape programmes and governance as we grow. Expect responsibility, not a membership card.

Apply by Email

Mentor or Volunteer

We need research mentors and operational volunteers — people who will show up on Zoom, keep records, and finish tasks. See open roles below.

View Open Roles

Partner With Us

Universities, departments, labs, NGOs, and agencies: we want joint mentorship, shared workshops, and co-branded pilots with clear MoUs — not logo exchanges.

Propose a Partnership

Support Our Work

We can receive lawful grants and donations. We will not invent impact numbers. Early support should fund a laptop, connectivity, training tools, and the first mentorship pilot.

Programme Coordinator

Communications Lead
